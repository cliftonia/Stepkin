## Description (4000)

Stepkin is a pocket pet from the nineties, rebuilt for Apple Watch.

A creature hatches on a tiny pixel screen on your wrist. It gets hungry. It
makes a mess. It wants the light off at bedtime and sulks if you forget. Look
after it and it thrives; neglect it and, like the toys you remember, it can
die.

What is new is the wrist. Stepkin reads your daily steps from Health, and
those steps are what make it grow. Walk every day and it evolves through five
stages into one of fourteen creatures, each with its own look and attacks.
Skip too many days and the goal drifts further away.

The world outside shows up on the screen too. With your permission, Stepkin
reads the local weather: rain, snow, fog, wind and storms play across the
scene, and real daylight decides when it is night.

- Feed meat and vitamins, clean up, heal injuries, switch the light at
  bedtime.
- Train in five-round sessions and take on opponents in battle.
- Fourteen creatures across five evolution stages, chosen by how you care
  and how much you move.
- Care reminders on the wrist, held until morning so nothing buzzes at 2am.
- A watch-face complication that shows your creature at a glance.
- A red battery-saver palette that follows Low Power Mode.
- Nothing leaves your watch. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Ask Siri to "Feed Stepkin" or "Check on Stepkin". The complication rises in
the Smart Stack while your creature needs care.

Steps and weather are optional. Without steps the creature still lives and
grows with time, more slowly than if you walk.

## App Review notes

Stepkin is a watch-only app: a virtual pet in the style of the 1990s
keychain toys. There is no account, no purchase, no network use beyond
WeatherKit, and no third-party SDK.

Two permissions are requested after a short walkthrough, and both are
optional:

- Health (read step count): the daily step total decides when the creature
  evolves. Declining it leaves the creature alive and growing with time,
  more slowly. Background step updates follow the same Health permission;
  there is nothing extra to grant.
- Location (when in use): fetches local weather through WeatherKit to draw
  the scene. Declining it removes the weather effects and uses the clock for
  day and night.

Every screen is reachable without granting either. The creature can die of
neglect after two days without food or care; that is the intended game
mechanic, matching the original toys, and a new egg follows.

To see evolution quickly: the first evolution requires 10,000 lifetime
steps. Adding step samples to Health on the paired iPhone is reflected on
the next refresh (about ten minutes, or reopen the app).
